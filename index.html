Privacy Policy (English)

WallPair is a couple wallpaper-sharing app for Android and iOS (com.clipper.wallpair). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: Google sign-in — email, display name, account ID
  • WallPair ID for partner pairing
  • Partner data: partner IDs, connection status, trusted settings
  • Wallpapers you choose or apply (may be stored in cloud for sync)
  • FCM token for push notifications
  • Basic usage needed to run the service (we do not sell your data)

2. How we use it

  • Authentication and account security
  • Partner pairing and wallpaper sync
  • Push notifications when the app is in the background
  • Abuse prevention (rate limits, reports, blocks)

3. Third parties

  • Google / Firebase — Auth, Database, Storage, Messaging, Functions
  • Pexels — suggested wallpapers & photographer credits
  • Unsplash — fallback suggested images

4. Permissions

Internet, notifications, photos/gallery, camera (QR), set wallpaper (Android).

5. Retention & deletion

Contact us to request account and cloud data deletion.

6. Children

Not directed at children under 13.
