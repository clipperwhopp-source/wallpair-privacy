1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WallPair (“WallPair,” “we,” “the app”) handles information when you use the WallPair mobile application for Android and iOS (package / bundle ID com.clipper.wallpair). We do not sell your personal information.

Data Controller

Data Controller: Aşkın Gündüz, individual developer of WallPair

Contact: clipper.whopp@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use WallPair, we may process account and authentication information, partner-connection data, photos and wallpaper delivery data, device and notification tokens, subscription-related status, and local app preferences. Details are in the sections below.

3. Account and Authentication Information

  • Google Sign-In (Android and iOS)
  • Sign in with Apple (iOS)
  • email address associated with the sign-in provider (Apple may provide a real or private relay email, depending on your Apple settings)
  • display name, when provided by the sign-in provider or set in the service
  • Firebase Authentication user ID (Firebase UID)
  • WallPair ID used for pairing
  • authentication state needed to keep you signed in

4. Partner Connections and Requests

  • partner connection status and partner identifiers
  • connection requests
  • approval / trust preferences (for example, whether wallpaper delivery may proceed without a separate in-app approval)
  • partner-related history maintained by the service for reconnecting and related features
  • wallpaper delivery and related activity records needed to operate pairing

WallPair currently provides disconnect and report options where available in the app. This policy does not claim that a separate Block feature is available.

5. Photos and Wallpaper Content

  • You may select images from your device to send or apply.
  • Selected images may be uploaded to Firebase Storage.
  • Image URLs and related delivery records may be stored in Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Images are shared only through the connected-partner workflow with your selected connected partner.
  • iOS: a received image may be saved to Photos. You must apply it manually as wallpaper. WallPair does not directly set the iOS system wallpaper.
  • Android: on supported devices, WallPair may use Android system APIs to apply home-screen and/or lock-screen wallpaper. Behavior may vary by device, Android version, manufacturer restrictions, or selected destination (home screen and/or lock screen).
  • Content already saved or applied on another device cannot be remotely removed.
  • The current account-deletion implementation does not automatically delete Firebase Storage wallpaper files. Those files may remain after account deletion.

6. Device and Notification Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token processing on iOS, as used through Firebase Messaging
  • notification delivery for partner requests, wallpaper alerts, and related service messages
  • device identifiers / tokens used for push delivery
  • notification permission choices on your device

Notification permission is optional. You may deny notifications and continue using WallPair. Notifications may not be delivered if permission is denied or disabled. Operating-system delays, device restrictions, network restrictions, battery optimization, or similar conditions may also affect delivery. Notification delivery is not guaranteed. You can later change notification permission in your device settings. Denying or disabling notifications does not prevent you from opening WallPair and checking partner requests or wallpaper activity in the app.

7. Subscription and Purchase Information

Where offered, WallPair may provide optional Premium access through auto-renewable subscriptions, including:

  • Monthly Premium
  • Annual Premium
  • Localized price, duration, and renewal terms are displayed by the store before you purchase.
  • Payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your platform.
  • RevenueCat manages entitlement and subscription status used by WallPair.
  • Your Firebase UID may be used as the RevenueCat App User ID so subscription status can be associated with your WallPair account.
  • Product, entitlement, transaction status, renewal / expiration status, and purchase history may be processed to provide Premium access.
  • WallPair does not receive full payment-card details.
  • Deleting a WallPair account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Purchase and subscription records may remain with Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat after you delete your WallPair account, according to their policies.

Local app data

The app may store on your device information such as language selection, onboarding state, a Premium-status cache, app preferences, and other necessary local state to operate the app.

8. How We Use Information

  • authenticate you and secure your account
  • create and maintain your WallPair profile and WallPair ID
  • enable partner pairing, requests, and wallpaper sharing
  • deliver push notifications you have permitted
  • provide and restore Premium subscription access
  • prevent abuse, investigate reports when reasonably possible, and protect the service
  • operate, maintain, and improve core app functionality

9. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the data and context, WallPair processes personal data where necessary:

  • to provide and perform the WallPair service requested by the user;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • for legitimate interests such as service security, fraud and abuse prevention, report handling, and service reliability, provided these interests do not override user rights;
  • based on user consent where consent is required, including optional device permissions controlled by the user.

This section describes possible legal bases in general terms. It is not a claim of universal legal compliance, certification, or that every basis applies in every jurisdiction or for every processing activity.

10. How Information Is Shared

  • with your connected partner, as needed for pairing and wallpaper delivery
  • with service providers that process data for us (listed below)
  • when required by law, legal process, or to protect safety, rights, or the integrity of the service

We do not sell your personal information.

12. Data Storage and International Processing

Information may be processed and stored on infrastructure operated by Google/Firebase, Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat, and related providers. This may involve processing outside your country. By using WallPair, you understand that such processing may occur as described in those providers’ policies.

13. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained while needed to provide the service.
  • Account data covered by the current deletion function is removed during the in-app (or verified support) deletion process.
  • Cloud wallpaper files in Firebase Storage are not automatically deleted by the current deletion implementation and may remain.
  • Transaction and subscription records may be retained by Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat.
  • Limited reports or security records may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, safety, legal compliance, or dispute handling.

14. Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account (scroll to the bottom), then confirm and reauthenticate when asked.

Public instructions are also available at https://wallpair.pages.dev/delete-account.

The current deletion process removes your Firebase Authentication account, WallPair profile, partner connection, partner requests, and related account-specific Realtime Database records handled by that flow. It does not automatically delete Firebase Storage wallpaper files. Deleting your WallPair account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

If you cannot access the app, contact clipper.whopp@gmail.com. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. Ownership verification may be required.

If you cannot access the app, contact clipper.whopp@gmail.com. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. Identity / ownership verification may be required.

Older versions of this policy described deletion only by email. In-app deletion is now the primary method; email remains available if you cannot use the app.

15. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the service, including HTTPS and authenticated access controls provided through Firebase and related platforms. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Children’s Privacy

WallPair is not intended for children under 13. Do not use WallPair if you are under 13, or if local law requires a higher minimum age without appropriate authorization. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. This statement is not a COPPA certification.

To exercise rights that require our assistance, contact clipper.whopp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Some rights may not apply in every situation, and some processing may continue where another legal basis or exception applies.

  • Mevcut hesap silme uygulaması, Firebase Storage’daki duvar kağıdı dosyalarını otomatik silmez. Bu dosyalar hesap silindikten sonra kalabilir.

İşletim sistemi gecikmeleri, cihaz kısıtlamaları, ağ kısıtlamaları, pil optimizasyonu veya benzer koşullar da iletimi etkileyebilir. Bildirim iletimi garanti edilmez. Bildirim iznini daha sonra cihaz ayarlarından değiştirebilirsiniz. Bildirimleri reddetmek veya kapatmak, WallPair’i açıp partner isteklerini veya duvar kağıdı etkinliğini uygulamadan kontrol etmenizi engellemez.
